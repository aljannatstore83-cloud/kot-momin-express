Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

Kot Momin Express is a local delivery service in Kot Momin, Punjab. Riders buy what you order from shops in the city and bring it to your door. Contact: 0345 7792869 (call or WhatsApp).

کوٹ مومن ایکسپریس کوٹ مومن (پنجاب) کی مقامی ڈیلیوری سروس ہے۔ رابطہ: 03457792869 (کال یا واٹس ایپ)۔

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number, home address and the delivery pin on the map.
  • Your location when you use the app, to check that you are inside our service area and to guide the rider.
  • Your IP address, a device ID and the phone type, each time you open the app.
  • Your orders: items, voice notes, photos, chat with the rider, bills, payment confirmation, ratings and complaints.
  • KYC (one time): your CNIC number, photos of the front and back of your CNIC and a selfie. These are locked (encrypted) on your phone before they are sent. Only the owner of Kot Momin Express can open them, with a separate KYC password.
  • Riders: location while they are online or delivering.

Why we use it

  • To take, buy and deliver your order, and to show you where your rider is.
  • To verify new customers and keep riders safe from fake orders, robbery and fraud.
  • To solve complaints and wrong bills.
  • If a crime happens (for example a rider is robbed), we may give the record to the police.

یہ معلومات آرڈر پہنچانے، نئے کسٹمر کی تصدیق، رائیڈرز کی حفاظت اور دھوکہ دہی سے بچاؤ، اور شکایات حل کرنے کے لیے ہیں۔ کسی جرم کی صورت میں ریکارڈ پولیس کو دیا جا سکتا ہے۔

Who can see it

  • Your rider sees your name, number, address, delivery pin, the order, your order history with us and whether you are verified. Riders never see your CNIC or KYC photos.
  • The owner of Kot Momin Express can see all records to run the service.
  • We do not sell your information to anyone.

Storage and your choices

Records are stored with Google Firebase. We keep them while you use the service and as long as needed for safety and fraud records. To ask us to remove your information, call or WhatsApp 0345 7792869. Records about fraud or crime may be kept.
