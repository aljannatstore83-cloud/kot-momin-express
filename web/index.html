Test the full flow on one phone

  1. Open Customer app, allow location, install it, and place an order.
  2. Open Rider app, install it, sign in with 03001234567 and PIN 4821, and accept the order.
  3. Follow the steps: confirm, bill photo, "I have arrived", then type the customer's 4-digit code.
  4. Open Admin with your admin PIN to see orders, customers, cash and security.
Demo mode: until Firebase is connected, orders stay on the phone where they were placed. Customer and rider apps on the same phone share them, so you can test everything on one phone.
