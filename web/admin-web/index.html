Service area (Kot Momin)

Drag the centre pin or tap the map to move it. Slide to change the radius.
km
The service area is a circle: the same distance (radius) from the centre in every direction, from 0.5 km up to 50 km. Customers outside it cannot open the app or place orders. Green dots are past delivery pins, orange dots are people who opened the app from outside the circle (they could not order). Default: 5.5 km around Kot Momin city centre.
